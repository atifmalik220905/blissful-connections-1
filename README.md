# Blissful Connections

Here is a complete website development prompt in English:

Create a modern, peaceful, spiritual, and conversion-focused website for a meditation and retreat brand named “OSHO® Bliss”.

The website should feel calm, premium, soulful, and trustworthy. Use a clean design with soft spiritual colors like white, beige, saffron, earthy orange, deep maroon, and natural green. The design should give a feeling of silence, healing, inner journey, meditation, nature, and retreat life in Rishikesh.

The main CTA should be WhatsApp. Add WhatsApp buttons in the hero section, service sections, trainer sections, footer, and sticky bottom/mobile CTA.

4. Facilitators Section

Create a section called Meet Your Facilitators.

Christine

Christine was born in Australia and has been on the path of inner exploration since 2008, when she began learning meditation at the London Buddhist Centre.

Previously a humanities teacher, later working in management and as Head of Faculty, she realised that the best way to make a difference in the world is to begin within. This understanding led her to move into more conscious work in 2021.

She is trained in pranayama, Soma Breath, and Osho Meditations. Over the last 5 years, she has guided breathwork and meditation sessions in London, Australia, India, and at the Osho International Meditation Resort in Pune, as well as online.

Her work has reached psychiatric hospitals, children facing removal from mainstream education due to behavioural challenges, corporate wellness programs, community relaxation sessions, meditation camps in Rishikesh, Ayurvedic training colleges in Kerala, and private therapeutic sessions in India and the UK.

Christine holds a nurturing and safe space for release, reconnection with the true self, and playful exploration of consciousness.

Sarju

Sarju was born in Gujarat, India, and has been a Sanyassin of Osho since 2008.

Previously an entrepreneur involved in an oil mill and tyre plant, he also worked in construction management in London.

While running his businesses in India, Sarju frequently stayed for long periods at Osho ashrams in Madhavpur, Mehsana, and Pune, where he was able to dive deeply within.

On his journey to find Zorba the Buddha, he discovered the value of Zen discipline and carries this principle in his work to help others explore and find their own truth.

Sarju began by informally teaching breathing techniques and meditation practices to friends for many years. In 2022, he expanded into facilitating Osho meditations and has since held intimate sessions in London, at the Osho International Meditation Resort in Pune, and across India. He also facilitates Meditation Camps in Rishikesh.
